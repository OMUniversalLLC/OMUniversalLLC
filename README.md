## About OM UNIVERSAL LLC

**OM UNIVERSAL LLC** is a Florida-based U.S. business focused on retail operations, operational efficiency, data-driven decision-making, and technology-enabled business management.

Our work supports convenience-store and fuel-retail operations, including the coordination of inventory, purchasing, vendor relationships, reporting, audits, and continuous process improvement.

## U.S.–India Operations

OM UNIVERSAL LLC's U.S. retail operations are supported through **Vivazen Bharata Services Pvt. Ltd.**, an India-based company located in **Gujarat, India**.

The India-based operations function focuses on back-office coordination, business intelligence, workflow execution, reporting, and operational analysis. This describes an **operations-support relationship** and does not imply that the Indian company owns the U.S. entity.

We aim to build practical systems that help teams:

Our public repositories are intended for suitable project documentation and non-confidential source code. **Credentials, API keys, private operational records, financial information, and employee or customer data must not be published.** Sensitive configuration belongs in appropriately protected systems and environment secrets.
